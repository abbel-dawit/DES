Payment technology

Payment acceptance, supplied and supported in Ethiopia

We supply, deploy, and support POS hardware and acceptance platforms for Ethiopian banks, microfinance institutions, and payment operators. Hardware is manufactured by NEW POS Technology and reaches Ethiopia through our partnership with Infinite Icon Technology; what DES adds is everything between the factory and a working estate.

22 devices

Terminals & devices

Android smart POS from a 165g pocket device to a 6.7-inch flagship, Linux and RTOS keypad terminals, LAN and PINPAD variants, plus sound boxes and Android cash registers.

7 platforms

Estate platforms

NewStore application marketplace with TMS, remote key injection, and NewViewer diagnostics, alongside SoftPOS tap-to-pay, Cloud MIS, and aggregation acquiring.

Certified

Compliance evidence

PCI PTS 6.x across the range and 7.x on the NEW9228, EMV Level 1 and 2 with contactless, and the international scheme kernels needed alongside domestic acceptance.

Unattended

Self-service

Embedded payment modules in two cutout sizes for kiosks, vending, parking, transit, fuel, and EV charging.

From purchase order to live estate

Specification, import and customs, key injection, and merchant handover are run as one sequence against an agreed rollout plan.

01

Specify

Acceptance profile, terminal mix, certification requirements, and connectivity per merchant segment.

02

Stage

Procurement, import, firmware and parameter loading, remote key injection, and asset tagging before dispatch.

03

Deploy

Site installation, merchant onboarding, acceptance testing, and handover, run in waves.

04

Sustain

TMS monitoring, remote support, spares pool, firmware cycles, and refresher training.

Specifications and certifications are as published by the manufacturer and are subject to change; current certificate numbers and expiry dates are confirmed in writing at the point of order, together with the evidence pack required for NBE submission.

Corporate training

Upskilling the teams who keep systems running

Every deployment we deliver comes with a training track. Technology only transforms an organization when the people inside it can own it, which is why no two curricula we run are identical.

Cohorts are typically 8 to 16 delegates. Larger populations run in waves so the institution keeps operating while its people are trained, and materials and delivery are bilingual as standard.

What the certified system changes for you

ISO 9001 is a system standard, not a training accolade. These are the specific controls the certified system puts around a joint programme.

Documented scoping

Every programme starts from a recorded needs analysis and agreed learning objectives, signed off before design begins.

Controlled materials

Course materials are version-controlled and reviewed, so two cohorts six months apart receive equivalent content.

Trainer competence records

Instructor qualification and subject currency are recorded and reviewed rather than assumed.

Measured feedback

Delegate evaluation and outcome data are collected every cohort and fed into the next revision.

Non-conformity handling

When something falls short there is a defined corrective process with an owner and a closure date.

Management review

Programme performance is reviewed at management level on a fixed cycle, with actions tracked.
