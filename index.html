Payments made simple!

Card payments should work every time. We make that happen for banks, microfinance institutions and payment companies across Ethiopia.

Our partners supply certified terminals. Our local team prepares, installs and services every device.

  1. Choose & order

    You tell us your needs. We recommend terminals and handle import and customs.

  2. Install & activate

    We install each terminal and load its security keys. Each merchant gets a short training.

  3. Connect & integrate

    We link terminals to your cash registers, ERP and accounting tools.

  4. Service & support

    We watch every device, repair faults quickly and keep spare parts in stock.

How do we work?

Every DES project follows four clear steps. You always know what happens next.

  1. Listen

    Understand your real needs

    We visit your offices and meet the people who do the work. We learn how things run today.

  2. Plan

    Design systems and skills together

    We design the system and the training plan at the same time. Both fit your budget and rules.

  3. Build

    Install while your team learns

    We install in stages with regular checks. Your staff practise on the system as it grows.

  4. Grow

    Support you long after launch

    Our local team stays available. We review your progress and plan the next improvements.

“Listen first. Plan together. Build with care. Grow for years.”
